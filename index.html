01 / Research Overview

Exploring chemistry for a sustainable future

Welcome to a research platform dedicated to advancing natural product chemistry and sustainable environmental chemistry. This platform explores the potential of natural resources, biomass and waste-derived materials in developing innovative solutions to contemporary environmental challenges.

The research presented here encompasses natural product valorisation, sustainable polymer development, circular bioresources, plastic biodegradation and environmental remediation, with an emphasis on resource efficiency and environmentally responsible chemical processes.

Beyond showcasing scientific publications and research interests, this platform serves as a knowledge-sharing space for students, researchers and academics. Through the Research Digest, visitors can discover scientific literature, emerging research developments and educational insights that encourage critical thinking, scientific curiosity and interdisciplinary collaboration.
